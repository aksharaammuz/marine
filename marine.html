Thinking Beyond the Usual Career Choices?

Every year, thousands of students complete Class 12 and face the same question: “What should I do next?” Engineering, medicine, or a regular degree aren't the only options.

If you are interested in travel, technology, teamwork and a challenging career at sea, the Merchant Navy could be worth exploring.

But how do you actually join the Merchant Navy after 12th? Here's a simple step-by-step guide.

01

Choose the Right Career Path

Your first step is choosing a course based on the role you want. Popular options include B.Sc. Nautical Science, Diploma in Nautical Science (DNS), and B.Tech Marine Engineering.

Nautical courses lead towards deck-officer careers, while Marine Engineering focuses on the technical and machinery side of ships.

02

Check Your Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the course you choose. Generally, courses such as Nautical Science and Marine Engineering require Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM).

Candidates must also meet prescribed medical and eyesight standards and other requirements set by the relevant maritime authorities and institute.

03

Clear the Entrance & Selection Process

For eligible courses, candidates may need to appear for the IMU-CET (Indian Maritime University Common Entrance Test).

Some shipping companies also conduct their own sponsorship or selection processes. Check the current requirements before applying.

04

Complete Maritime Training

After admission, students undergo approved pre-sea training and work towards the required certifications.

This training prepares students for the responsibilities and practical challenges of working onboard a ship.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

✓ Choosing a course without checking eligibility
✓ Selecting an institute without checking approval or recognition
✓ Choosing Merchant Navy only because of salary or travel

Is Merchant Navy Right for You?

The career can offer international exposure, career growth and a unique working environment.

However, it also means spending extended periods away from family and adapting to a disciplined, demanding lifestyle.

So, don't choose it only because you saw an impressive salary online!

Ready to Take the First Step?

A career in the Merchant Navy can be rewarding when you choose the right course and understand the journey ahead.

Research your options, verify eligibility and prepare with the right guidance before taking your first step towards life at sea.
